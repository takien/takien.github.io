<p>When my program got crashed or not responding I usually end its process through Task Manager.<br/>But, because of some cases &#8211;e.g. viruses, or registry restriction&#8211; my Task Manager cannot be accessed by pressing Ctrl + Alt + Del.<br/>Tips:<br/>Type in Command Prompt or Run dialog :<span style="font-style: italic;"> taskkill /im processsname.ext  /f</span> and press enter.<br/>Replace processname.ext with the name of your program, it could be be firefox.exe for Firefox, or winword.exe for Microsoft word.</p>
