<p>When defragmenting the hard drive, make sure you turn off any screensaver you  may have as this will prevent the defragmentation process from completing. You  can turn off the screensaver by right-clicking on a blank area of the desktop,  going down to &#8216;Properties&#8217;, clicking the &#8216;Screensaver&#8217; tab, and choosing &#8216;None&#8217;  from the &#8216;Screensaver&#8217; drop-down box.</p>
