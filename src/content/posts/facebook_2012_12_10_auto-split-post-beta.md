<p>Auto Split Post - Beta</p>

<p>Easily split post into multiple page with Title link, not just a number. It's better than default WordPress split functionality. Also support "AUTO SPLIT", for example you want split post on each <h2> tag. However you can still use <!--nextpage-->; to manually split a post.</p>

<p>Feature:</p>

<p>Selectable style format: dropdown, tab, link list</p>

<p>Beta version:</p>

<p>- dropdown belum work</p>

<p>- tab, belum ane bwt CSS nya</p>

<p>Why split a post?</p>

<p>- Faster pageload.</p>

<p>- Increase pageview.</p>

<p>- Prevent Google Webmaster Tools to say "Article too long" :D</p>

<p>- Increase Adsense revenue. Yes, you can insert  Adsense code on every part of your article.</p>

<p>- Reduce bounche rate. Bounche rate is the percentage of visitor entering your site and just leave rather than continue viewing another page on the same site.</p>

<p>- Biar keren, kyk situs-situs Hardware review :ngakaks</p>
