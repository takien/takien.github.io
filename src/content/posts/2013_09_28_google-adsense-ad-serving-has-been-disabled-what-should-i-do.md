<p>If you found your website doesn&#8217;t show up AdSense ad, your website may has been disabled by Google because your website to be in violation of AdSense program policies. In many case, your website can be re-enabled to show AdSense if you follow the right step.</p>
<h2>So, what should I do?</h2>
<ol>
<li>Don&#8217;t panic.<br/>
Be in panic will not resolve any problem.</li>
<li>Read notification email and see what caused the ads disabled.<br/>
Google always send notification about your account change, if your site disabled read the email carefully and follow the step they gave.</li>
<li>Immediately make change.<br/>
Make change your affected URL or page. Double check all other URL in the whole website if necessary. Delete or edit it.</li>
<li>Use Policy Violation Appeal to submit your request.<br/>
Lately you can resolve this issue directly from Adsense dashboard. Browse the policy violations section, you will find your affected website, click on the &#8216;Mark as Resolved&#8217; and select what changes you have made and click OK.</li>
<li>Just wait, if you luck your site will be re-enabled to serving Adsense again.</li>
</ol>
<h2>How to prevent similar occurence in the future?</h2>
<ol>
<li>Check your email frequently. Ususally Google give 3 days to make changes before the site to be disabled.</li>
<li>Always make good and original content, read Google guide carefully, do not cheating.</li>
</ol>
<p>This post does not represent Google&#8217;s rules and is not an official guide. Always use Google support as your reference.<br/>
