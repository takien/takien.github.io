						<p>Hello, recently I found this amazing free desktop application for prototyping named Evolus Pencil.</p>
<p>Here is some features of the software:</p>
<ol>
<li>Free and opensource, you don&#8217;t have to spend money to get it.</li>
<li>Cross-platform, it&#8217;s available for Linux, Windows and Mac OSX, and even as Firefox Extension</li>
<li>Create wireframe for your design easily with pre-built shapes, including common desktop and mobile UI.</li>
<li>You can also use it to draw diagram and flowcart.</li>
<li>Well-crafted application, I have used it many times and no problem so far.</li>
<li>Export drawing to various format, including PDF, SVG, HTML, PNG and OpenOffice document (ODT)</li>
<li>Easy, you do not need to read documentation to use it.</li>
<li>Light and fast, it runs smoothly on my 4GB systems.</li>
<li>Built-in clipart browser, you can insert free clipart from Openclipart.org directly to your canvas.</li>
<li>And manymore.</li>
</ol>
