<h2>How to install?</h2>
<p>Copy <code>php.xml</code> to <code>plugins\APIs</code> under your Notepad++ installation directory, overwrite existing, backup first if necessary.</p>
<p><strong>Note:</strong></p>
<p>By default, Notepad++ doesn&#8217;t enabled autocomplete. You have to enable it through <code>Settings</code> -&gt; <code>Preferences</code>. Go to Auto-completion tab, check on <code>Enable autocompletion</code> and <code>Functions parameter hints</code>. You don&#8217;t need to restart Notepad++.</p>
