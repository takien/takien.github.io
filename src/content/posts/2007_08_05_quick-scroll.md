## Quick Scroll

<p>To scroll down a lengthy web page quickly in Internet Explorer, simply press the space bar on your keyboard and it will scroll one screen length down (the Shift key in conjunction with the space bar will scroll one screen length up).</p>

## open link in new window/tab

<p>Worried if a link will open in new tab/window or not?<br/>Or you don&#8217;t want to lose your current page? <span style="font-style: italic;">Internet Explorer:</span> Ctrl + Click = Open link in new window. <span style="font-style: italic;">Mozilla Firefox:</span> Ctrl + Click = Open link in new tab. <span style="font-style: italic;">Mozilla Firefox:</span> Shift + Click = Open link in new window. <span style="font-style: italic;">Opera :</span> Shift + Click = Open link in new tab. <span style="font-style: italic;">Opera :</span> Ctrl + Click = Open link in current window <img src="/images/2007/08/icon_smile.gif" alt=":)" title="" class="rounded-lg my-6 max-w-full h-auto shadow-md" loading="lazy" /> </p>

## quick google search (again)

<p>Finding some words / phrase when browsing? Wanna search another resource related to that words / phrase in Google? Select these words / phrase using your mouse, right click and select menu &#8216;Search Google for &#8220;your selected words/phrase&#8221;&#8216;. ## Mouse Gestures on Opera Browser

<div class="text">
<p>Mouse Gestures is currently only available on Opera Browser. It&#8217;s useful to navigate faster by letting your mouse movements control the navigation. Browse between pages, open and close windows, and navigate links with quick mouse movements.</p>
<p>Tip:</p>
<p>Hold down the right button at the same time by moving your mouse to the left will bring you to the previous page.</p>

</div>

## Paste and Go

<p>Paste and Go (Ctrl + B) is the one of Opera features, doing this will paste a links you have copied into the address bar and immediately execute the link. Pressing Enter is no longer needed.</p>

## Show only this frame

<p>Firefox browser will let you tho show only the frame content sources.<br/>To do so, right click on the frame and choose Show only this frame. You know that some people hate framed website, including me 😉 </p>

## Hard Refresh

<p>Sometime you have to &#8216;hard refresh&#8217; the browser to view new changes in your website contents. Holding Ctrl key at the same time you hit the Refresh button or when pressing F5. Works on all browsers.</p>
