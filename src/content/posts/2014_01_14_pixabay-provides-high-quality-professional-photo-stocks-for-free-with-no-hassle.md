<p>On the Internet world, anything can be happened, buy crappy things for hundreds dollars, or get good stuff for free. Let say in finding image, yep, if you're like to find high quality photograph for your web design or simply for your desktop wallpaper, Pixabay is the right choice.</p>

<p>Knowing this site about six or more months ago, pixabay has become my favourite photo stock provider for my need. The layout is easy to navigate and fast to load, stock images growing from time to time. Images are available in a variety of sizes and get it with just one click.</p>

<p>Perhaps you think that this is the same as the other image providers with creative common license. However, maybe you're wrong, because the images available here are very nice, not a random taken photograph. And the most important, you can freely use any image from this website in digital and printed format, for personal and commercial use, without attribution requirement to the original author.</p>

<p>Just like most sites, <a href="http://pixabay.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Pixabay</a> place ads on the site for their revenue. But do not worry, you can get rid the ads by the way contribute there, upload 10 images of your work. Think of it as a portfolio, if you are a photographer. Not only photos, in addition, pixabay also provide stock for vector and clipart. And still, they are free.</p>
