<p>Google recently releases &#8216;official&#8217; Google Adsense Plugin for WordPress. The Google Publisher Plugin enables you to easily use Google&#8217;s products &#8211; including AdSense and Webmaster Tools &#8211; with your WordPress site.</p>
<p>Adding AdSense ads usually requires you to generate snippets in AdSense and paste these into your site, or to manually edit PHP files. This plugin lets you place AdSense ads using a simple point-and-click UI.</p>
<p>Features of this beta version of the Google Publisher Plugin:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Google AdSense</strong>: Earn money by placing ads on your WordPress website. The plugin links your WordPress site to your AdSense account and makes it very easy to place ads on your site. Use AdSense with the Google Publisher Plugin.</li>
<li><strong>Google Webmaster Tools</strong>: Webmaster Tools provides you with detailed reports about your pages&#8217; visibility on Google. The plugin allows you to verify your site with Webmaster Tools with only one click. Use Webmaster Tools with the Google Publisher Plugin.</li>
</ul>
<p>Wanna try? Get it here <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20160316170706/http://wordpress.org/plugins/google-publisher/" target="_blank">http://wordpress.org/plugins/google-publisher/</a></p>
