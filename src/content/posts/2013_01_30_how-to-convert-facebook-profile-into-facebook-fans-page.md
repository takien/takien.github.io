						<p>Having Facebook profile for your community, company or organization could be bad idea. Why? Because Facebook profile is intended for individual people to interact with your friend and family. Especially when your community become bigger and bigger. You will find it difficult to accept all friend requests, everyday.</p>
<p>If you reading this and you still have Facebook personal account for your community or company, it&#8217;s time to switch. Migrate it into fans page.</p>
<p>Maintaining a personal account for anything other than an individual person is a violation of Facebook&#8217;s terms. If you don’t convert your noncompliant account to a Page, you risk permanently losing access to the account and all of its content.</p>
<p>Here is how to convert Facebook profile into Facebook fans page.</p>
<p>1. Login into your Facebook account.</p>
<div id="attachment_1204" style="width: 310px" class="wp-caption aligncenter"><a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20150423190918/http://img.takien.com/2013/01/facebook-fans-page-1.jpg"><img src="/images/2013/01/facebook-fans-page-1-300x177.jpg" alt="Facebook login page" title="" class="rounded-lg my-6 max-w-full h-auto shadow-md" loading="lazy" /></a><p class="wp-caption-text">Facebook login page</p></div>
<p>2. Visit this link: <strong>http://www.facebook.com/pages/create.php?migrate </strong>to begin migration.<strong><br/>
</strong></p>
<p>3.  You will be redirected into Facebook page creation. Select category which is best match to your company or organization.</p>

<p><strong>Note:</strong></p>
<p>When you convert your personal account to a Facebook Page, only your current profile picture will be transfered and your friends and followers become people who like your Page. Your account&#8217;s username will become the username for your Page, and the name associated with your personal account will become your Page&#8217;s name. If you want your Page to have a different name, consider creating a new one.</p>
